Editorial Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Goal Zone is an independent football publisher. Our goal is to provide useful, accurate and original football coverage for readers.

Original content

We write our own summaries, analysis and explanations. We do not reproduce full articles from Sky Sports, ESPN, Fabrizio Romano or other publishers.

Sources

We may check major football publishers, official club websites, competition organisers and recognised journalists. Source links are provided where appropriate.

Transfers and rumours

We distinguish confirmed transfers from reports and rumours. A transfer is not labelled confirmed unless there is reliable confirmation. Reported information may change.

Corrections

If we publish an error, readers can contact us at goalzonefootballnews@gmail.com. Please provide the article title and the correction.

Advertising

Goal Zone may display advertising in the future. Advertising does not determine our editorial decisions.
