FOOTBALL KNOWLEDGE

Learn the game

What is a high press?

A high press is an attempt to win the ball close to the opponent's goal by moving players forward together and reducing passing options.

How to read a transfer rumour

Look for the source, whether talks are reported, whether an agreement exists and whether either club has officially confirmed the move.

What makes a good match report?

A useful match report explains the result, important incidents and why the match developed as it did, while separating facts from opinion.
