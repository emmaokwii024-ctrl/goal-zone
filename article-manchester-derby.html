13 September 2026 • Goal Zone match report

Manchester United and Manchester City share points in goalless derby

The first Manchester derby of the new Premier League campaign ended without a goal at Old Trafford.

Manchester United and Manchester City could not find a breakthrough in a tight derby at Old Trafford. The 0-0 scoreline means both sides leave the fixture with a point.

For the managers, the match offered a useful test of how their teams handle a high-pressure fixture. United will want to build on their defensive resilience, while City will look for more attacking sharpness in the next round.

Goal Zone's view: derby matches often become tactical battles, and this one was decided by the absence of a finishing moment rather than a lack of intensity.
