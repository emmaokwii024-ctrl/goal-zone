Liverpool 0-0 Fulham: points shared at Anfield

Liverpool were held to a goalless draw as Fulham produced a disciplined performance and ended their losing run.

Liverpool controlled important periods of the match but could not find the finishing touch needed to break Fulham down. The visitors defended with discipline and earned a valuable point at Anfield.

The result is significant because Liverpool need to convert home pressure into goals if they want to stay close to the early leaders. For Fulham, the clean sheet provides a useful foundation for the matches ahead.

Goal Zone's view: goalless draws can still reveal plenty about a team. Liverpool's next step is to improve the quality of the final action, while Fulham can take confidence from their defensive organisation.
