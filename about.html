GOAL ZONE

About Us

Goal Zone is a football news and information website created for fans who want clear, useful and engaging coverage of the beautiful game.

We cover football stories from Uganda and around the world, including transfer developments, match stories, player features and football analysis.

Our approach

We aim to publish original, easy-to-understand content and clearly distinguish confirmed information from reports and rumours. We value accuracy, transparency and respect for our readers.

Our mission

Our mission is to give football fans a simple place to follow important stories while supporting interest in Ugandan and international football.
