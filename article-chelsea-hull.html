Chelsea 2-2 Hull City: two points dropped at Stamford Bridge

Chelsea scored twice but could not hold on as Mohamed Belloumi's double helped Hull City maintain their unbeaten start.

Chelsea made a quick start through Morgan Rogers, but Hull responded strongly. Mohamed Belloumi scored twice in the first half to turn the game around before João Pedro restored parity after the break.

The main lesson for Chelsea is defensive consistency. Their attacking players were able to create pressure, yet conceding twice to the same opponent left the hosts with only a point.

For Hull, the draw is another sign that their early-season form deserves attention. Remaining unbeaten away from the pressure of Stamford Bridge gives the promoted side confidence going into the next round of fixtures.

Goal Zone's view: Chelsea have enough attacking quality to challenge strong opponents, but their defensive structure will need to become more reliable if they want to turn entertaining games into wins.
