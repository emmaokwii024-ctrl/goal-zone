ARSENAL

12 September 2026 • Goal Zone match report

Arsenal beat Sunderland 2-0 and keep the pressure on the leaders

Arsenal left the Stadium of Light with three points after goals from Bruno Guimarães and Bukayo Saka.

Arsenal kept their strong early-season league run going with a 2-0 victory at Sunderland. Bruno Guimarães broke the deadlock in the second half before Bukayo Saka converted a late penalty.

The result shows Arsenal's ability to remain patient in a difficult away match. Sunderland stayed competitive for long periods, but Arsenal eventually found the moments needed to decide the contest.

Goal Zone's view: the important feature of Arsenal's start is not simply the number of wins but their ability to manage difficult matches. The next challenge is maintaining that consistency as the schedule becomes more demanding.
